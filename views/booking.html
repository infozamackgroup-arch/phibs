Booking, Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Booking & Payment

Booking is confirmed after full payment or 50% deposit.

Balance is to be paid before check-in. Keys will be released after full payment.

Please pay only to the official Phibs Luxury Homes account.

2. Caution Fee

A refundable caution fee of ₦50,000 is required.

It will be refunded within 24 hours after checkout if there is no damage or missing item.

3. Cancellation & Refund

– 48 hours or more before check-in: 100% refund (minus bank charges)

– Less than 48 hours before check-in: 70% refund

4. Date Change

You can reschedule once for free if you tell us at least 48 hours before check-in.

Reschedule is subject to availability.

5. Early Checkout

If you leave early, we will refund you for the unused nights, minus 1 night as admin fee.

6. Refund Process

Refunds are sent within 2-3 working days to the same account you paid from.

By making payment, you agree to this policy.
