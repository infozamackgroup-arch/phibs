ABOUT PHIBS LUXURY HOMES

Hospitality with comfort at its heart.

Phibs Luxury Homes is a Nigerian shortlet apartment company offering luxury homes for short and extended stays in Abuja. Established in Nigeria and incorporated in June 2022, we exist to give guests a calm, comfortable and private place to stay, whether for business, a short getaway or an extended visit.

Every suite is prepared with care, and our team is on hand from your first enquiry to checkout, so you can settle in and feel at home.
